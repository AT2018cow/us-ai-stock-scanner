# Candidate interpretation policy (draft — no scores, no thresholds)

## KEEP (generic core, all models)

- Share-count mechanics (`shares_yoy`, dilution): model-free.
- Freshness/missingness discipline: absent evidence never scores.
- Identity verification + UNRATED fail-closed.

## NEEDS SPECIALIST INTERPRETATION (research now, score later or never)

- Banks/brokers: leverage, coverage, margins, OCF, accruals, multiples —
  every row marked doubtful/inapplicable in the matrix. Candidate inputs:
  NII ex-provisions, CET1, charge-offs, P/TBV. No cross-model comparison
  until validated on a distinct sample.
- REITs: profitability, leverage, coverage, multiples via FFO/AFFO,
  Debt/EBITDAre, fixed-charge coverage. Current B grades stay
  non-comparable.
- Broker revenue vocabulary (GS class): tag-alias research first.

## KEEP AS UNRATED (do not force-rate)

- Subsidiary/thin entities without measurable operating facts (XOM class)
  until entity selection is resolved.
- Foreign/unsupported reporting models (currency gate already enforced).

## ENTRY (frozen v1)

- No state remapping. Per-name unmet-condition tables stand as the
  diagnostic product. Silent-band inventory (RS [-10%,-3%), ret60
  [-8%,0), drawdown/vol/sma50-slice evidence gaps) is a candidate for
  future evidence-completeness work, explicitly not a policy change.
